**Description:**
AgriNET brings your field sensor data to your phone.

See all your sensors on a map, tap any of them to open its readings, and follow soil moisture, weather station data and other sensor types over time with clear charts. Summed charts and data lists help you compare fields and spot trends at a glance.

- Interactive map of all your sensor sites
- Charts and history for each sensor type
- Scan a QR code on field equipment to jump straight to its sensor
- Show your current position on the farm map

AgriNET is for AgriNET customers: sign in with the account provided by your administrator.

**What's New (1.0):** First release on iOS.

- **Notes:**
  AgriNET is a companion app for existing AgriNET sensor customers; accounts are issued by the administrator (no in-app registration). Use the demo account above to see the map, sensor charts and lists. Location is used only to show the user's position on the map. The camera is used only to scan QR codes on sensors/equipment. Sensors themselves are hardware read through the AgriNET cloud, so no hardware is needed to review.
